# Remotos LATAM

Status: active but flaky — last verified 2026-10-05
URL: https://remotoslatam.com
Account: not needed to browse.
Type: job board

## Search
Use the search box on the home page (direct `/trabajos?q=` URLs have returned 404). Filters are unreliable and
results include stale or closed postings and Portuguese-market roles. Empty results are a normal outcome.

## Known issues
- 2026-10-01: some "listings" are agency placeholder pages with dozens of unrelated roles. Open each one and
  confirm it has a real job description before applying.
