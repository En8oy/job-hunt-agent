# Computrabajo

Status: active — last verified 2026-10-16
URL: https://mx.computrabajo.com (country subdomain)
Account: required. Stores a CV under "Mi CV" → "Currículum en Word/PDF".
Type: job board

## Search
Search by title + location or "Remoto". Postings don't expose a stable per-job URL, so dedupe by company +
position (the tracker does this when `--url` is omitted).

## Apply
One-click apply with the stored CV, sometimes with killer questions. Status appears under "Mis postulaciones"
("CV visto", "En proceso", "Finalista", "Descartado"). Many postings are "Importante empresa" (anonymous):
record the company as shown.
